Questions?

Is Salona right for my salon?+

If you have a licensed women's salon in Riyadh, you can apply for the Founding 10 programme
The programme covers one branch per salon, and acceptance depends on the programme terms and seats available

Can I add WhatsApp and phone bookings?+

Yes. Add them in Salona and follow them with all your other bookings in the same schedule

Does Salona guarantee new clients?+

We can't guarantee a number of bookings, but being listed in the app gives clients another way to discover your services and book with you

What happens after the free trial?+

Nothing is charged automatically
If you decide to subscribe, you turn it on yourself at the founding price of 149 SAR a month before VAT. It renews on the same day each month, and you can stop renewal any time from your dashboard

Is there a commission on bookings?+

No commission on bookings for your first 12 months. We'll tell you about any change at least 30 days before it applies
Clients pay your salon directly, and Salona never handles the money

How do I contact support?+

On WhatsApp, in Arabic, daily 8 AM to 9 PM at 059 898 4523.
